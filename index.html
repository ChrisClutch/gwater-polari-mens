The Creed

A drink that keeps its own counsel.

Polari takes its name from the private vocabulary once spoken among men who had reason to speak carefully. It was not a slogan. It was a way through the world.

We made a water in that spirit. The can is heavy in the hand, the label is quiet, and the room it enters gets a little better for its presence. Nothing about it is shouted. Everything about it is felt.

Some things are for everybody. This is not one of them — and that is the compliment.
The Founders
The Series

The can is a canvas.

Four times a year, Polari commissions a gay artist — painter, photographer, printmaker, designer — to make the next label. The edition is numbered. When the run is gone, the can is gone. What's left is the work, the artist, and what it paid for.

10%
of every can sold returns to the artist — in addition to the commission. Not a gesture. An arrangement.

The Making

Three things, done properly.

No. 01

The Water

A single artesian source. Lightly mineral, softly effervescent. No sugars, no sweeteners, no claims — just a clean, considered drink meant to be had slowly, ideally with someone.

No. 02

The Can

Matte black aluminum, letterpress-quality label, oxblood foil. Heavy for its size. Built to look like it belongs on the counter of a good bar, and to photograph like the object it is.

No. 03

The Mark

A single dot, in oxblood red. It's on the can, on the case, on the coaster at the bars that stock us. The only flag we fly. If you know what it means, welcome.
